# Guided interface rollout

The new interface lives at `/new`. Classic remains at its existing URLs. Both use the same authenticated APIs, active household, financial records, subscription, and feature flags. No financial data is copied when switching.

## Delivery order

1. **Implemented — reversible foundation:** classic/new switch, remembered browser preference, protected routes, shared account selection, responsive navigation, light/dark/system themes, and feedback collection. Feedback review is at `/admin/interface-feedback`.
2. **Implemented — daily routine:** guided home; one balance pass for cash accounts and cards; envelope scan, transfers, and direct balance adjustments. Checking balances, importing transactions, and allocating income remain distinct actions.
3. **Implemented — payday and monthly planning:** editable allocations against remaining monthly funding targets; preview available-to-save impact; monthly and annual target editing; dedicated income planning at `/new/income`. Goal-linked settings open the new goal editor. Premium/feature gates control advanced suggestions and category-type edits.
4. **Implemented with existing task screens — weekly routine:** CSV upload, mapping, queue review and approval, transactions, and reports stay within the new shell. These share components with classic to preserve validation, duplicate detection, merchant descriptions, and categorization. Weekly/monthly check-in links preselect a report period. Refine the interior of these screens after feedback on the core routine.
5. **Implemented locally — core setup:** `/new/accounts/manage` adds bank/cash accounts and credit cards, edits details and inclusion, and updates credit limits alongside available credit. `/new/budget/manage` creates and edits envelopes using purpose-based planning, notes, optional priority, and archive/restore. These secondary pages are linked from the daily tools and monthly plan without adding sidebar clutter.
6. **Implemented locally — goals and income buffer:** `/new/goals` walks through purpose, tracking source, plan, and a setup preview. Paths cover debt payoff, purchases/events, safety reserves, and retirement. Supports new/reused envelopes, dedicated cash accounts, investment values, and debt with an optional payment envelope. Edits targets, pace, notes, tracking status, and existing debt/payment-envelope links; separates current, paused, and reached goals; explains ongoing use and offers envelope activity and in-place money adjustments. Buffer actions preview reserve/available balances, record audit entries, and lead from releasing money to allocation. Account relinking, deletion, and loan/investment setup and balance updates remain in classic.
7. **Next — broader coverage:** use the priorities below. Classic tools remain accessible throughout; revise the order as feedback shows which tasks force users to switch back.

### Next design priorities

1. **Recurring bills and subscriptions:** organize confirmed commitments around what is coming up and what changed. Separate detected suggestions from confirmed recurring charges. Since many people import weekly, distinguish “not yet seen in imported transactions” from a missed or unpaid bill; do not invent payment status from stale imports.
2. **Spending drilldowns and trends:** answer “what changed?” and “does my plan need adjusting?” with category/merchant history and comparisons that lead back to envelope planning. Preserve compact date filters and the new theme's chart palette.
3. **Other commitments and the longer view:** pending checks/reserved cash, then loans, assets, and net worth. Keep spendable budget money separate from investments and future money. Household/settings workflows follow, with clear permissions and contextual entry points.

This order favors completing the everyday budget before expanding the navigation. Setup remains separate from review, imports remain separate from balance updates, and no new screen silently moves money.

## Interaction contract

- Sidebar links and direct task URLs open standalone pages. Check-in guidance requires an explicit, valid `checkin` query parameter for that task. Leaving a check-in through the sidebar ends the guided visit without changing its saved progress. Standalone balance and monthly-plan edits use ordinary Save actions that stay on the page and never mark a check-in step reviewed.
- Daily, weekly, and monthly check-ins guide attention. A numbered progress header and persistent completion action lead through each routine: balances → envelopes; imports → spending → envelopes; reflection → monthly plan → enabled goals/buffer. Completing a review never requires moving money or achieving a perfect budget.
- The primary action saves pending balance/plan edits and continues only after every save and the authoritative refresh succeed. Save & stay is available separately. Invalid input, partial failures, and refresh errors keep the current step open. Jumping into a later step circles back to unfinished earlier steps instead of claiming completion.
- Every routine ends at `/new/check-in/complete` with a review summary, recorded available to save, remaining negative envelopes, and a clear Done action. Revisit individual tasks or choose Review again to restart just the review progress. Opening the completion URL with unfinished steps offers Continue instead of claiming success. Monthly completion waits for planning feature availability.
- Check-in progress is private to this browser, user, and household; it is not financial or import history. It resets by calendar day, Monday-start week, or month. It does not claim that every bank was imported.
- Weekly import links preserve their explicit check-in context through upload, mapping, queue review, and transaction detours. Standalone and classic imports remain standalone.
- Transactions now use a dedicated new-look workspace. Search matches original bank descriptions and merchant names; filters include multiple envelopes/accounts/merchants, all selected tags, dates, income/expense, and missing envelope/account assignments. The money-in/out totals cover the complete result set, and category-filtered totals use only matching split amounts. Rows link to envelope pages; a side panel shows account, original description, split assignments, tags, and original import details, with previous/next review and existing edit/delete actions. CSV import is primary; manual entry remains available. Batch envelope assignment explicitly replaces splits; partial failures remain selected. Duplicate detection and other bulk tools retain classic links.
- The transaction review read endpoint scopes both list and summary queries to the active household. Related filters run before pagination; summary totals page all matching records and fail on any incomplete page. This leaves classic queries and money-writing APIs unchanged. Client requests cancel stale results; native browser history keeps filter/search focus and supports transaction deep links and Back. The phone view uses compact filters and readable cards, with the same light/dark theme tokens.
- Reports, category details, tag reports, and transactions share a compact date-range control. Existing report presets sit beside a readable range summary; the summary opens start/end calendars and clearing controls. Open-ended ranges remain supported. Reports keep the date, category, and system-category controls on one desktop row and two compact mobile rows. Changing categories preserves the chosen dates.
- Failed report requests show a retry action instead of empty/stale charts and block guided completion until all report data loads. Changing filters cancels outdated requests. Planning-setting failures also offer retry rather than implying optional tools are disabled.
- Account balances are entered manually. Cards use **available credit**, copied directly from the provider and sent unchanged to the existing API. Owed is calculated as saved credit limit minus available credit. The limit is read-only in the daily balance pass and editable in the new account setup page. Bank balances and cards have separate labeled sections. Credits above the limit are shown as a positive card credit; dashboard totals retain the existing zero floor on amounts owed. Pending charges/holds are reflected only to the extent the provider includes them in available credit.
- Account setup previews the change to available to save. Editing a cash account's metadata never sends a stale balance; card settings send credit limit and available credit together only when changed, preserving statement balances and other fields. Balances and envelope funding remain separate. History, statement balances, and permanent account removal remain available in classic.
- Setup links opened from a check-in carry a whitelisted `from` value and offer a return to the same daily, weekly, or monthly step. Direct setup visits stay standalone, and setup never completes a check-in. Monthly-plan rows deep-link to a specific envelope editor with `edit=<id>`.
- Envelope setup asks for a purpose: monthly spending, yearly costs (annual amount divided by 12), or a reserve (balance target plus planned monthly contribution). New envelopes start at zero; editing never sends a current balance. Disabled category-type/priority features preserve existing advanced fields. Goal, buffer, and system categories stay with their dedicated tools. The editor previews the resulting monthly plan against income.
- Archive/restore preserves category IDs and history. The new UI requires a zero balance before archiving an ordinary envelope and rechecks its latest balance before the write. Nonzero envelopes link to review/move money; existing archived envelopes can be restored. This guard is a UI workflow, not a new atomic database invariant. Archive and restore use the existing bulk-archive endpoint. Ordering and permanent deletion remain in classic.
- A transfer preserves total envelope funds. Direct balance adjustments change available to save but do not count as monthly funding. Payday allocations increase monthly funding.
- Activity links, Review buttons, and home attention links open the review dialog on **Activity**. It uses the existing category audit endpoint, newest recorded changes first, with signed envelope amounts, before/after balances, funding month, transfer counterpart, import context, and a separate transaction date. The bounded scroll area pages through older activity, preserves loaded history on paging failures, and offers retry/refresh. Transaction details open without leaving the envelope; full classic details remain linked. Move money and Set balance are adjacent tabs, with inputs preserved while reviewing activity. Current-month funding/spending totals remain visible above the history.
- Dialog destinations use `/new/budget?envelope=<id>&tab=activity|transfer|adjust`. Activity and Review links explicitly select Activity; Move money and home Cover overage shortcuts explicitly select Transfer. Set balance is also directly linkable. Tab changes replace the URL without resetting form drafts; opening pushes a history entry. URL changes (including browser navigation) control the open envelope/tab, while closing clears only those parameters and preserves check-in context. Missing/unknown tabs default to Activity.
- Envelope names link to a dedicated `/new/budget/[id]` page. Its balance-first overview separates money left, monthly budget, and recorded monthly funding; the funding shortcut filters the full audit trail. Activity can be filtered to funding, transfers, transactions, or adjustments before pagination, with before/after balances and transaction details in place. Six months of category-split expenses are shown separately from refunds/income; every month links to its transactions. Movement, direct balance adjustments, and plan/notes editing reuse existing editors without leaving the page. Purpose-specific context respects yearly costs, reserves, and goals. Detail links preserve check-in context; switching interfaces opens the matching classic category.
- Category insights verify active-household ownership and page all transaction splits in stable ID order, failing rather than displaying partial totals. Calendar-month boundaries are explicit, transaction counts are distinct, and sums use integer cents. No new money-writing endpoint or migration is needed.
- The envelopes page leads with actual **Money left**, **Monthly budget**, and **Funded this month**. There is no spending-derived "left in plan" amount or net-refund headline. Cash bars compare the current balance with the monthly budget for everyday envelopes, the annual budget for yearly costs, or the balance target for reserves. Each bar has a visible percentage, reference amount, and reference label, with a nearby legend defining the fill as money left; captions retain percentages above 100% while the track caps at full. Negative balances omit the bar and state the amount to cover. Goal cash remains a plain amount: its monthly contribution is not its overall goal target. Spending/refunds remain in Activity. Negative balances show the exact amount to cover; everyday balances above the monthly budget show the excess without treating savings as spare cash. Filters cover shortfalls and extra cash to review. Funding is displayed as its recorded amount without a "still to fund" directive, since an envelope may already hold enough carryover or adjusted cash; allocation decisions stay on the funding page. The header links to funding with Available to save alongside it. Mobile cards retain all three numbers.
- Monthly spending now pages the scoped, date-filtered transaction query in stable ID order so more than 1,000 transactions cannot silently undercount progress. A failed page fails the whole response. Focused regression coverage: `npx vitest run src/lib/__tests__/envelope-overview.test.ts src/lib/__tests__/monthly-spending-pagination.test.ts`. Desktop light/dark and 390px browser checks cover money-left bars, the three displayed amounts, negative cash, carryover, filters, sorting, empty searches, and Activity/Transfer deep links. Disposable test examples were removed after QA.
- Potential surplus applies only to ordinary monthly expense envelopes. Accumulation, target, goal, and buffer balances must not be presented as spare money just because they are high.
- Goal creation starts a new envelope at zero. Account-linked goals explicitly preview excluding the entire existing account from budget totals; they do not transfer money. Debt edits preserve the original starting debt. Only envelope contributions become monthly funding targets. Pausing tracking does not remove a funding target or release money. Unchanged past target dates are omitted from updates so overdue goals can still be edited.
- Goal details distinguish envelope money left from all-time categorized spending. Money adjustments reuse the existing envelope editor without leaving the goal/check-in URL. Monthly-plan edit links return to the monthly plan. Goals and buffer remain feature/premium gated; source relinking, deletion, and loan management have explicit classic fallbacks.
- Buffer add/release uses the existing endpoints with write-access checks, strict amount validation, an available-to-save check for additions, and compare-and-set protection on the buffer balance. A failed/uncertain response is never automatically retried. Releasing only increases available to save; allocation is a separate explicit step. The available-total check is not a database-wide lock: simultaneous writes to other envelopes/accounts remain part of the broader atomic-money-write rollout gate. Audit logging retains the existing best-effort behavior.
- Suggestions are editable. Payday uses money already reflected in recorded balances, never assumed future income. Ordinary envelopes use the current monthly plan minus recorded funding; target-balance envelopes use the remaining balance gap without subtracting prior funding twice. Suggestions leave goals manual until their completion state is part of this screen.
- Financial mutations refresh authoritative server totals. Failed or partial saves are visible; no simulated Undo or success state.
- On/off options use accessible slide switches in the new interface, including income inclusion, paycheck-arrival confirmation, report visibility, and shared transaction/import settings. Labels and keyboard Space toggle the same boolean values; read-only/saving states remain disabled. Shared screens keep classic checkboxes on classic routes. Multi-select menus and transaction row selection retain their checkbox semantics.
- Income planning shows included/excluded sources, estimated monthly net income, a gross/deduction/tax breakdown, and the gap to monthly funding targets. The editor accepts gross pay per paycheck/month/year, ordinary percentage tax entry, fixed/percentage pre-tax deductions, biweekly planning basis, inclusion, and optional retirement asset links. Display-unit changes preserve the underlying annual value. It uses the existing income API and calculation helpers; saving never records a deposit or allocates money. Last-source removal is offered as exclusion because the legacy API re-creates a default source if all records are deleted. Scenario planning is intentionally omitted from the new interface. Monthly check-in links return to the same plan step; the income page does not complete that step.
- Viewers retain read-only access. Existing API authorization remains authoritative.
- New feedback is saved before site admins are alerted through the existing system-notification service. Alerts include a short excerpt, type, source page, submitting user, and budget account, with a direct admin-only link to the full submission. Email follows each admin's System Notifications preference; bell notifications remain available if email is disabled or fails. Notification bells are available in the new interface and admin header. Retrying the same feedback ID does not send another alert. Delivery errors use the existing server/delivery logs and never undo saved feedback.

## Local development and release

Goal workflows require migration `129_goal_workflows.sql`, applied to the test project only. Apply it before deploying code that reads the new relationships. It adds optional purpose/payment-envelope metadata, investment-linked goals, and a transactional, write-authorized setup RPC. Existing goals retain a null purpose and unchanged progress behavior. Guided setup uses a creation key so retrying an identical submission cannot create another envelope.

Goal workflow contracts:
- Purchase envelopes count leftover plus net all-time categorized spending. Safety reserves count only leftover and reopen after spending below their target. Retirement contribution envelopes count only net categorized contributions, with reserved cash shown separately. Investment goals track current recorded asset value, without changing spendable cash or forecasting returns.
- Reusing an ordinary envelope preserves money/history and converts its funding settings to a monthly goal target. The preview explains historical transactions and recommends a fresh envelope for a new contribution period.
- Debt progress always comes from the card/loan balance, never its payment envelope. Linking an existing payment envelope changes no money or funding target. A new payment envelope is allowed for loans/cards excluded from totals; included cards already deduct all debt, so setup explains the extra earmark if an existing envelope is linked. Inclusion flags are never silently changed by debt setup.
- Dedicated bank accounts are explicitly excluded from totals in the reviewed setup transaction. Failed setup rolls back category/account changes. Every linked source is checked against the active household; ordinary envelope links reject archived/system/buffer/other-goal categories.
- Purpose-specific progress and instructions also appear in classic goal details and the progress API. Help documentation explains the actual accounting and import workflow.

### Production rollout stages

1. Internal/test-project review of this branch, including CSV imports, permissions, financial edits, and failure recovery.
2. Merge to `main` and use the existing migration/deployment workflow to offer the opt-in new interface. Keep classic as the default, all classic routes, and the return switch. Gather task-specific feedback before adding more surface area. Retain `codex/guided-budget-interface` for further iteration.
3. Address API atomicity/idempotency below and expand beta based on verified success with shared households, viewers, and premium/free accounts. Add cross-device workflow metadata only after its semantics are settled.
4. Consider changing the default interface only after critical classic tasks have equivalent coverage and users no longer need to switch back for their regular routine. Retiring classic is a separate decision.

## Follow-up risks

Existing transfer and allocation APIs perform multiple database updates rather than one transaction. This interface reuses their semantics, does not automatically retry uncertain writes, and refreshes before another action. Atomic RPCs, idempotency keys, and concurrent-edit protection across both interfaces should precede a broad rollout. Monthly funding failure after an allocation must be shown explicitly so the user does not reallocate accidentally.

Check-in progress and interface preference are browser-local initially. Cross-device progress, cohort rollout controls, and feedback triage improvements are separate metadata work; none should alter the shared financial model.
